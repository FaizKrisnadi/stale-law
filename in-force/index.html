    What this does

    A statute can remain in force after a provision changes. This curated prototype records selected instruments and provisions from a research pilot.

    Search by citation or words in a recorded provision. Compare the two pilot dates and follow the source links. The chain is a research note, not a complete historical consolidation.

    The index is a fixed snapshot, last checked 9 November 2026. It cannot establish law after that date or reconstruct article wording for an earlier date.

    Every row carries the document it was checked against. Where the index has not read something, it says so.

    What it will not do

    • It holds nine instruments and seventeen provisions. It will not guess about a tenth.
    • It reads numbers, years and article references. It does not understand a sentence.
    • Some treaty claims rely on secondary sources, and the entry into force date for ACFTA is unresolved in the register.
    • It is not legal advice. For a filing, open the document named in the provenance block.
