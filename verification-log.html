E3 Superseded"The 1994 China–Indonesia BIT protects the investor."Terminated effective 31 Mar 2015; survival expired 31 Mar 2025. KWM, 2 Sep 2026; UNCTAD (termination date)The headline error. A 2026 measure cannot generally be brought under that BIT.
E3 Superseded"RCEP gives an ISDS right."Chapter 10 has eighteen articles and none provides investor–state arbitration; Art. 10.18 commits the Parties to discuss it. RCEP Chapter 10Wrong forum. The live route is the ACFTA Investment Agreement.
E4 Right instrument, overstated rule"The ACFTA gives you FET, NT, MFN and protection against indirect expropriation."Art. 7(2)(a) defines FET as not denying justice in legal or administrative proceedings. Art. 4 omits admission. Art. 6 preserves non-conforming measures. "Indirect" does not appear in Art. 8 as retrieved.This is the error that survives fixing the date. It is the reason the entry exists.
E4"MFN gets you the better standard from another treaty."Art. 5(2)–(4) carve out future agreements, existing preferences, intra-ASEAN preferences, and dispute-resolution procedures.The MFN route is much narrower than the textbook version.
E2 Valid provision, wrong question"Article 7 covers the quota cut / export retention / forestry closure."Art. 7 is a direct nationalisation / taking-of-ownership rule. Stretching it to regulatory measures is interpretation.Overstates the domestic claim; understates the work needed to characterise indirect expropriation.
E2"Article 7(3) means Indonesia agreed to arbitrate the whole dispute."Art. 7(3) sends the compensation amount to arbitration if there is no agreement. Lawfulness of the taking is elsewhere.Wrong scope for a clause.
E5 Missed temporal statusThe same paragraph answers March 2020 and November 2026.In March 2020 the survival clause was still running; in November 2026 it is not.Fails the paired probe even if one date was half-right.
E4"Art. 32(4) lets the investor pick arbitration and sue in court."Art. 32(4) sends a foreign-investor dispute to international arbitration "yang harus disepakati oleh para pihak" — which both parties must agree to. Articles 7 and 32 are absent.The inference is backwards: entry was rewritten, protection was left alone. A 2026 answer that quotes the 2007 Art. 7 is quoting live text.

The honest claim

AutoClaw produced a register in which every load-bearing citation is either attached to a URL or marked open. The protocol caught a terminated treaty, a missing ISDS chapter, a stretched taking clause, and — the one that matters most — a live treaty described more generously than its own text allows.

That last error is the interesting one. It is invisible to anyone who checks whether the instrument is in force and stops there. The instrument was in force. The sentence describing it was still wrong.

That is the product. It is not "the AI knows Indonesian investment law."
