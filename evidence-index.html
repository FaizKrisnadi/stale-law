Two things the closing changed in the argument, not just in the register:

  1. Art. 32(4) is conditional. A dispute with a foreign investor goes to international arbitration "yang harus disepakati oleh para pihak" — which must be agreed by the parties. Domestic law supplies no standing consent to arbitrate. A claim needs a treaty that does. That is a stronger reason to care about the ACFTA than the memo originally gave.
  2. The omnibus rewrote entry and left protection alone. UU 6/2023 changed Arts. 2, 12, 13, 18 and 25 — scope, the closed list, MSME partnership, facilities, licensing. It did not touch Art. 7's nationalisation guarantee or Art. 32's dispute route. The 2007 protection text that a 2026 answer quotes is still the right text, for a reason now on the record.

Method note, so the next reader can check it

D5 and D8 were closed by reading the amending instruments' own article lists, not a consolidated text. That is the same thing a practitioner does with the salinan, and it is stronger than trusting a consolidation. If a consolidated UU 25/2007 or UU 5/1986 is published later, it should agree; if it does not, the mismatch is itself the finding.
