9 November 2026

Treaty position at this date

Some protections in the 1994 bilateral treaty may still apply to qualifying investments.

Articles I–XII continued until March 2025 for investments made before termination. Arbitration under Article IX(3) covers only the amount of compensation for expropriation. The ASEAN–China agreement is also relevant.

The ASEAN–China Investment Agreement is one potential route.

External sources report that the 1994 treaty’s survival period ended in March 2025. Any claim depends on the investment, the facts and jurisdiction.

01The findingWhy the treaty text matters
01 / Finding

Two errors, stacked

A source check must establish both the relevant date and what each provision actually says.

Reg. 001

External sources report that the 1994 China–Indonesia BIT terminated on 31 March 2015 and its ten-year survival clause expired on 31 March 2025; the termination notification was not retrieved.

The ASEAN–China Investment Agreement was also in force by 2020. Its provisions have their own scope and qualifications, which must be assessed separately.

Article 7(2)(a) refers to fair and equitable treatment as “the obligation of each Party not to deny justice in any legal or administrative proceedings.” The text defines FET by reference to denial of justice; the application to particular facts requires analysis. Article 4 limits national treatment to management, conduct, operation, maintenance, use, sale and liquidation — admission and establishment sit outside it. Article 5 carves future agreements and intra-ASEAN preferences out of MFN. Article 6 preserves non-conforming measures. Article 3(2) covers investments made before or after entry into force, but binds no Party for acts that predate it.

A memo that says “the ACFTA gives you FET, NT, MFN and protection against indirect expropriation” is technically quoting the summary. It is not quoting the treaty.

05The methodHow the pilot checks its sources
05 / Method

The protocol

Six steps. Skip the second and you get a fluent memo about the wrong year.

  1. Fix the as-of date“What is the law” is not a question. “What was the law on 9 November 2026” is.
  2. Ask the pairRepeat it at an earlier date. Some answers change with time; others should stay the same.
  3. Retrieve the instrumentCommentary finds the treaty. It does not replace it.
  4. Quote, then glossVerbatim in the original language. Never paraphrase inside the quotation marks.
  5. Mark the gapIdentify missing sources and unresolved interpretations. A source-backed text does not by itself settle jurisdiction or a real dispute.
  6. Write the error logRecord possible errors and the source used to examine each one; label hypothetical examples clearly.
What this is not This is a worked pilot of a research design on whether regulatory agents cite law in force. It is not the study, not advice, and not a claim that any model always fails. The scenario is constructed; every instrument cited is real.
