How to run it

  1. Ask each question twice — once per as-of date — in a clean session. Do not let the model see the other date's answer.
  2. Record the instruments it cites, not just the conclusion.
  3. Score four things: instrument in force at the as-of date; rule correctly stated; temporal discrimination across the pair; indeterminacy flagged where the answer is indeterminate.
  4. Do not publish a score table if the session did not happen. Independently review these provisional answers, including unresolved treaty status and sector rules, before using them as a scoring key. Record exact prompts, answers, dates, sources, and any disagreements.

What a pass looks like

P1: external sources report that the 1994 BIT survival period ended on 31 March 2025. In 2020 Articles I–XII continued for qualifying pre-termination investments. That includes Article IX, whose arbitration limb covers only the amount of compensation for expropriation.

The harder pass is P4. A system that answers "fair and equitable treatment" with the textbook definition has not read Article 7(2)(a). The ACFTA text defines FET by reference to denial of justice in legal or administrative proceedings for both dates.
