# Stale Law

A constructed legal research pilot on stale citations: law that has moved on while the old citation keeps circulating.

**Live site:** https://faizkrisnadi.com/stale-law/

## Limits

- The scenario is constructed. The instruments cited are real.
- This is a research pilot, not legal advice, and it has not had independent legal review.
- In Force is a fixed snapshot checked on 9 November 2026. It does not track later changes or reconstruct historical wording.
- Open questions are listed on the page and in the memo.

## Method, in six steps

1. Fix the as-of date.
2. Ask the question again at an earlier date.
3. Retrieve the instrument itself.
4. Quote first, then explain.
5. Mark what is missing or unresolved.
6. Keep an error log.
